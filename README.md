# Remix of Milemarker Motorcare Calendar

I want to make an engine oil change and tire maintenance tracking app for car owners. Users can enter their vehicle details (vehicle type, model, plate number, current mileage) and record their engine oil changes and tire changes. They can also store details such as the oil type, tire type, replacement date, and mileage at the time of service.



The app should include a personal maintenance calendar that helps users know when their next engine oil change or tire replacement is due. Users can view past maintenance records and upcoming maintenance dates in a calendar view. The system should estimate the next service date or mileage based on the last maintenance record and the recommended service interval. Users should be able to customize maintenance intervals and receive reminders when a service is approaching or overdue.



The problem this app tries to solve is that car owners often forget when they last changed their engine oil or tires, especially when they own multiple vehicles or do not keep maintenance records. Forgetting regular maintenance can affect vehicle performance, safety, and long-term maintenance costs. Paper records and manual reminders can also be inconvenient and difficult to track.



Make the app simple, practical, and easy to use. Focus on the core user journey: add a vehicle, record an engine oil change or tire change, view maintenance history, check the personal maintenance calendar, and see when the next service is due. If users have multiple vehicles, allow them to manage each vehicle separately. Make the core experience quick and clear, and avoid unnecessary features. The app is designed for individual car owners, not vehicle service shops.
